# How to Track Your Order and Shipment

Track real-time shipment status, carrier milestones, and estimated delivery dates for all your Acme Store purchases.

## Steps

1. Log in to your account at acme-store.example.com and go to 'Order History'.
2. Find your order using the order number (#ACME-12345) and click 'Track Package'.
3. View current shipment milestones: 'Order Placed', 'Shipped', 'In Transit', or 'Out for Delivery'.
4. Click the carrier tracking number (UPS, FedEx, or USPS) to view detailed geolocation scans on the carrier portal.
5. If checking out as a guest, visit acme-store.example.com/track and enter your order number and billing zip code.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### When does package tracking information become active?
Carrier tracking numbers activate within 24 hours of package handoff from our warehouse to the delivery carrier.

### What does 'Out for Delivery' status mean?
'Out for Delivery' indicates that the package is on the local delivery vehicle and will arrive at your address before 8:00 PM local time.
