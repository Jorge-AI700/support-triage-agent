# How to Modify an Existing Order

Modify item quantities, product variations, or remove items from a pending order before our warehouse begins fulfillment. Once an order is being packed, modifications cannot be made.

## Steps

1. Sign in to your account at acme-store.example.com and open 'Order History'.
2. Select the pending order you wish to adjust and click 'View Details'.
3. Click 'Modify Order' next to the items you want to change.
4. Adjust quantities, remove unwanted items, or select alternative sizes or colors.
5. Review your updated cart total and click 'Save Changes' to confirm the modifications.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I add new items to an existing order?
You can add items as long as the order status remains 'Pending'. If the order has already moved to 'Processing', please place a separate new order on acme-store.example.com.

### Why is the 'Modify Order' button missing?
If the modification button is unavailable, your order has already been sent to our fulfillment center for packing and can no longer be edited.
