# How to Place an Order on Acme Store

A comprehensive guide on searching our product catalog, adding items to your shopping cart, and checking out smoothly.

## Steps

1. Browse catalog categories or enter keywords into the search bar at acme-store.example.com.
2. Click on a product to view item details, specifications, available sizes, colors, and pricing.
3. Select your options and click 'Add to Cart', then click 'View Cart & Checkout' in the cart slide-out.
4. Enter your shipping address, choose your desired delivery speed, and review your order summary.
5. Enter payment details, apply any promotional discount codes, and click 'Place Your Order'.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I complete an order as a guest without creating an account?
Yes. Acme Store supports guest checkout. You only need to provide an email address for order confirmation and shipping updates.

### Where do I enter a promo code or gift card during checkout?
On the Checkout Payment page, locate the 'Gift Card or Promo Code' field, enter your code, and click 'Apply' to see your revised total.
