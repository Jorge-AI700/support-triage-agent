# Managing Your Newsletter Subscription

Subscribe to receive promotional coupons, weekly deals, and product release updates, or unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time.

## Steps

1. To Subscribe: Scroll to the footer of acme-store.example.com, enter your email address into 'Stay Connected', and click 'Subscribe'.
2. To Unsubscribe via Email: Open any marketing email from Acme Store and click the 'Unsubscribe' link located at the bottom of the message.
3. To Unsubscribe via Account: Log in at acme-store.example.com, go to 'Account Settings' > 'Email Preferences', and uncheck 'Promotional Newsletters'.
4. Click 'Save Preferences' to update your communication settings.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Will unsubscribing from newsletters cancel my order notifications?
No. Unsubscribing from marketing emails will never suppress critical transactional messages such as order confirmations, tracking alerts, or security notices.

### How quickly does an unsubscribe request take effect?
Unsubscribe requests are applied immediately in our system, though please allow up to 24 hours for scheduled marketing campaigns to fully cease.
