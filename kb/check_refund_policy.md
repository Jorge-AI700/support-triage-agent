# Refund and Return Policy

Review Acme Store's 30-day money-back guarantee, return requirements, inspection procedures, and payout conditions for online and in-store purchases.

## Steps

1. Visit acme-store.example.com/refunds to review full policy details and terms.
2. Verify that your item was delivered within the last 30 calendar days.
3. Ensure returned merchandise is in original, undamaged condition with all accessories, manuals, and original packaging intact.
4. Initiate your return through 'Returns & Refunds' in your account dashboard to obtain a prepaid return shipping label.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What situations qualify for an immediate 100% refund?
Defective products, items damaged during shipping, incorrect merchandise sent, or orders canceled before shipment qualify for a complete refund including original shipping charges.

### How long does it take for a refund to post to my bank account?
Once your returned item arrives at our facility and passes inspection (typically 1 to 2 business days), refunds are issued immediately. Banks post funds within 3 to 5 business days.
