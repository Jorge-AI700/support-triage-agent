# How to Update Your Shipping Address

Update the delivery destination for an active order before dispatch or change your default shipping address for future Acme Store purchases.

## Steps

1. Log in to your account at acme-store.example.com.
2. Go to 'Order History' and select the pending order requiring an address update.
3. Locate the 'Shipping Address' section on the order summary page and click 'Edit Address'.
4. Enter your updated street address, apartment or suite number, city, state, and zip code.
5. Click 'Update Address' to save the new delivery destination for your shipment.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I change the delivery address after an order ships?
Once a carrier tracking number has been generated, address corrections cannot be made through our site. Contact the carrier directly (UPS, FedEx, or USPS) to request package rerouting.

### Can I change the destination country on a pending order?
Destination countries cannot be changed on existing orders due to tax and shipping recalculations. Please cancel the current order and place a new one with the international address.
