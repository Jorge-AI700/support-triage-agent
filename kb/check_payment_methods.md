# Accepted Payment Methods

Acme Store supports a wide selection of convenient, secure payment methods for purchases made through our website, mobile platform, and retail locations.

## Steps

1. Add desired merchandise to your shopping cart and proceed to Checkout at acme-store.example.com.
2. At the 'Payment Method' step, view the available payment options.
3. Choose your preferred payment method: Major Credit/Debit Cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover), PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, or Acme Store Gift Cards.
4. Enter your card details, account credentials, or gift card claim code.
5. Optionally select 'Save Payment Method' to safely store credentials for one-click checkout on future orders.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I split payment across multiple credit cards?
Our checkout system supports split payments only between an Acme Store Gift Card and one secondary payment method such as a credit card or PayPal.

### Are my credit card and banking details secure?
Yes. Acme Store uses bank-level 256-bit SSL encryption and strict PCI-DSS Level 1 compliance to ensure your payment details are never compromised or stored in plain text.
