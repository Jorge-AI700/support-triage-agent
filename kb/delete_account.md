# How to Delete or Close Your Account

Permanently close your Acme Store account and remove your personal information in compliance with privacy regulations.

## Steps

1. Log in to your account at acme-store.example.com using your current credentials.
2. Click on your profile icon and select 'Account Settings'.
3. Navigate to the 'Privacy & Security' tab and scroll down to 'Account Closure'.
4. Click 'Delete Account' and review the notice regarding the loss of order records and rewards.
5. Enter your password to verify your identity and click 'Confirm Permanent Deletion'.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can an account be restored after it has been deleted?
No. Account deletion is permanent and irreversible. All saved addresses, stored payment methods, and historical order receipts will be deleted.

### Can I delete my account if I have an active order or pending return?
No. Accounts cannot be closed while there are in-flight orders, pending deliveries, or active return refunds. Please wait until all transactions settle.
