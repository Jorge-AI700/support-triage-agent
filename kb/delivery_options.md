# Delivery Methods and Shipping Speeds

Explore our shipping options, delivery speeds, carrier partners, and rates for orders shipped across the United States and internationally.

## Steps

1. Add items to your cart and proceed to the checkout screen at acme-store.example.com.
2. Enter your destination shipping address to display available delivery methods and estimated arrival dates.
3. Choose 'Standard Shipping' (3 to 5 business days, free on orders over $50).
4. Choose 'Expedited Shipping' (2 business days) or 'Overnight Express' (next business day delivery) for time-sensitive orders.
5. Choose 'In-Store Pickup' if you prefer to collect your items for free at a local Acme Store retail location.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does Acme Store offer free shipping?
Yes. Standard shipping is completely free on all domestic orders totaling $50 or more before taxes.

### What carriers does Acme Store use for package delivery?
We partner with major carriers including UPS, FedEx, and the United States Postal Service (USPS) depending on the shipping method selected and package weight.
