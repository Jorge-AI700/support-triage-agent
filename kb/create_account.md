# How to Create an Acme Store Account

Set up a free Acme Store customer account to enjoy expedited checkout, shipment tracking, order history access, and members-only discounts.

## Steps

1. Visit acme-store.example.com and click 'Register' or 'Sign In' in the top header navigation.
2. Click the 'Create an Account' button on the sign-in modal.
3. Enter your full name, a valid email address, and choose a secure password (minimum 8 characters).
4. Review and accept the Acme Store Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
5. Click 'Create Account' and click the activation link sent to your email to verify your address.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Are there any fees or subscriptions required to create an account?
No. Creating an account at Acme Store is completely free with no recurring fees or maintenance charges.

### Can I sign up using Google or Apple single sign-on?
Yes. You can click 'Continue with Google' or 'Continue with Apple' on the registration screen for instant account setup.
