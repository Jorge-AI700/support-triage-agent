# How to Switch Between User Accounts

Seamlessly toggle between personal, family, and business profiles on Acme Store without losing active browser sessions.

## Steps

1. Locate the user profile icon in the upper-right corner of the acme-store.example.com navigation header.
2. Click the user icon to open the account dropdown menu.
3. Select 'Switch Account' from the list of options.
4. Choose from your previously saved profiles or click 'Add Another Account'.
5. Enter the login credentials for the secondary account and click 'Sign In' to switch active sessions.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Will switching accounts combine shopping carts or order histories?
No. Each account retains an entirely separate shopping cart, saved payment methods, address book, and order history.

### How do I remove an account profile from the quick-switch list?
In the account menu, click 'Manage Accounts' and select 'Remove' next to the profile you wish to sign out and disconnect.
