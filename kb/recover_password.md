# How to Recover or Reset Your Password

Reset a forgotten password or access credential to regain access to your Acme Store account securely.

## Steps

1. Go to the Acme Store login screen at acme-store.example.com/login.
2. Click the 'Forgot Password?' link located directly beneath the password input box.
3. Enter the email address registered with your Acme Store account and click 'Send Password Reset Link'.
4. Check your inbox for an email from support@acme-store.example.com titled 'Reset Your Acme Store Password'.
5. Click the secure reset link inside the email, enter your new password, confirm it, and click 'Save New Password'.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What should I do if the password reset email does not arrive?
Check your spam or junk mail folders. If the email is not found within 5 minutes, ensure the email entered exactly matches your registered account.

### How long does the password reset link stay active?
For account security, password reset links remain valid for 60 minutes from generation. After 60 minutes, a new reset request must be submitted.
