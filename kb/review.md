# How to Submit Product and Service Reviews

Share your authentic feedback and product ratings to help fellow shoppers make informed purchasing decisions on Acme Store.

## Steps

1. Log in to your account at acme-store.example.com and go to 'Order History'.
2. Locate the delivered item you want to rate and click 'Write a Product Review'.
3. Select an overall rating from 1 to 5 stars (with 5 stars being the highest).
4. Write a descriptive review headline and detail your experience with product quality, fit, and performance.
5. Optionally upload photos or videos of the product, then click 'Submit Review'.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does it take for a submitted review to appear publicly?
Reviews undergo automated moderation to filter spam and offensive language and are published on the product page within 24 to 48 hours.

### Can I edit or delete my review after publishing?
Yes. Navigate to 'My Reviews' under your account profile at any time to revise your written review or change your star rating.
