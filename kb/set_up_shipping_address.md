# How to Add and Manage Shipping Addresses

Save multiple delivery addresses in your Acme Store address book for fast, convenient checkout for home, office, and gifts.

## Steps

1. Log in to your account at acme-store.example.com.
2. Click your profile icon in the top header and select 'My Account'.
3. Select 'Shipping Addresses' or 'Address Book' from the account navigation menu.
4. Click 'Add New Address' and enter the recipient's full name, street address, apartment/suite, city, state, zip code, and telephone number.
5. Check the box marked 'Set as Default Address' if desired, then click 'Save Address'.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How many shipping addresses can I save in my address book?
You can store up to 10 distinct shipping addresses in your Acme Store account for personal, business, and gift deliveries.

### Does Acme Store deliver to PO Boxes and military APO/FPO addresses?
Yes. We ship to standard PO Boxes and APO/FPO military addresses via standard USPS shipping methods.
