# Estimated Delivery Times and Transit Windows

Understand delivery timeframes, tracking updates, and when to expect package arrival following dispatch from our fulfillment centers.

## Steps

1. Review your order confirmation email for the estimated delivery window calculated at time of purchase.
2. Once your order dispatches, locate your tracking number (e.g., ACME-TRK-98765) in the shipping confirmation email.
3. Visit acme-store.example.com/track or the carrier's tracking page to see real-time transit scans.
4. Note our standard transit windows: Standard (3-5 business days), Expedited (2 business days), Overnight (1 business day).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the daily cutoff time for same-day shipping?
Orders placed Monday through Friday before 2:00 PM EST ship the same day. Orders placed after 2:00 PM EST ship the following business day.

### What should I do if my package has not arrived past the estimated date?
If your shipment is delayed more than 48 hours beyond the estimated delivery date, contact support@acme-store.example.com with your tracking number for an immediate carrier inquiry.
