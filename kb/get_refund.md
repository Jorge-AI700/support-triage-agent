# How to Request a Refund

Step-by-step guidance on initiating product returns, reporting non-receipt or defective goods, and receiving money back.

## Steps

1. Log in to your account at acme-store.example.com and visit the 'Returns & Refunds' portal.
2. Select the order containing the items you wish to return.
3. Choose a return reason (e.g., defective merchandise, item not as described, or changed mind) and select refund to original payment method.
4. Download and print the prepaid return shipping label provided by Acme Store.
5. Pack the item securely in its original packaging, affix the label, and drop off the package at an authorized carrier location.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Are return shipping labels free for customer returns?
Return shipping is 100% free for defective, damaged, or incorrectly shipped items. Standard preference returns have a flat $4.99 fee deducted from the final refund.

### How will the refund be issued?
All refunds are automatically credited back to the original payment method used during checkout (credit card, PayPal, or gift card).
