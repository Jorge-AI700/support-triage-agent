# How to Speak with a Live Human Agent

Connect directly with a live customer care representative when automated help articles and virtual assistants are insufficient.

## Steps

1. Visit acme-store.example.com and launch the Live Chat widget in the bottom corner of your screen.
2. In the chat prompt, type 'Speak with agent' or select 'Connect to Human' from the assistance menu.
3. Remain in the chat window while our system queues the next available support specialist (typical wait < 2 minutes).
4. Alternatively, dial 1-800-555-ACME on your phone, press 0, and say 'Customer Service Representative' at the voice menu.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the average wait times to reach a human agent?
Average queue wait times are under 2 minutes for online live chat and between 3 to 5 minutes for telephone support during normal operating hours.

### Can an agent call me back rather than having me wait on hold?
Yes. If telephone queue hold times exceed 5 minutes, our automated phone system will offer you a callback option while retaining your priority in line.
