# Troubleshooting Payment and Checkout Errors

Resolve card declines, authorization failures, billing address mismatches, and checkout errors when shopping at Acme Store.

## Steps

1. Check card details: Confirm that the card number, expiration date, and 3- or 4-digit CVV security code were typed correctly without spaces.
2. Confirm billing address: Make sure the billing street address and zip code match the exact billing address on file with your bank or credit card company.
3. Verify funds and card limits: Ensure your account has sufficient available credit or balance and that online transactions are permitted by your card issuer.
4. Try an alternative payment method: Switch to an alternative option such as PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, or a different credit card.
5. Contact our billing team: If issues persist, call 1-800-555-ACME or email support@acme-store.example.com for checkout assistance.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why do I see a charge on my bank statement if my order failed?
A temporary authorization hold may appear on your bank statement following a failed attempt. This is not an actual charge and will automatically disappear within 24 to 48 hours.

### What should I do if my payment was declined due to fraud protection?
Contact your bank or card provider to authorize the transaction from Acme Store, then retry completing your order.
