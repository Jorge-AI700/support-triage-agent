# How to Track Your Refund Status

Check the progress of returned items, monitor inspection approvals, and verify when refund credits will be posted to your payment method.

## Steps

1. Sign in to your Acme Store account at acme-store.example.com.
2. Navigate to 'Returns & Refunds' under your Account dashboard.
3. Select your return claim using your return tracking number (e.g., #RET-77654) or original order number (#ACME-12345).
4. Review the return progress: 'Package in Transit to Warehouse', 'Return Inspected & Approved', or 'Refund Issued'.
5. Once status shows 'Refund Issued', check your bank or credit card statement within 3 to 5 business days for the posted credit.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How many days does it take for a refund to post to my original payment method?
Once Acme Store approves and issues the refund, credit card companies and financial institutions typically post funds within 3 to 5 business days.

### What happens if the original card used for payment has expired or been cancelled?
Your financial institution will automatically reroute the refund to your replacement card or open account. Contact your bank if you no longer hold an account with them.
