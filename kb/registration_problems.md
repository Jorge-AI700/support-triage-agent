# Troubleshooting Registration and Sign-Up Issues

Overcome common account creation problems, including verification link failures, duplicate email warnings, and password validation errors.

## Steps

1. If you see an 'Email already registered' notice, use the 'Forgot Password' link at acme-store.example.com/login to recover your existing account.
2. Ensure your password meets security standards: at least 8 characters, containing at least one uppercase letter, one number, and one special character.
3. Clear your browser cookies and cache, or attempt registration in a private/incognito browsing tab if the submit button is unresponsive.
4. Check your email spam folder if you have not received your account confirmation and activation email.
5. If registration still fails, contact support@acme-store.example.com with the exact error message or screenshot for manual account provisioning.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can two family members share one account email address?
No. Each individual Acme Store account requires a unique email address to ensure account security and order privacy.

### What should I do if my account activation link has expired?
Attempt to log in with your email and password at acme-store.example.com/login to trigger an updated activation link to your inbox.
