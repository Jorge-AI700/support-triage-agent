# How to Cancel an Order

Learn how to cancel an order placed with Acme Store before it enters warehouse fulfillment. Once an order has shipped, cancellation is no longer possible online and the package must be returned instead.

## Steps

1. Log in to your Acme Store account at acme-store.example.com.
2. Navigate to 'Order History' under your Account dashboard.
3. Locate the order you wish to cancel using your order number (#ACME-12345) and click 'Order Details'.
4. Select the 'Cancel Order' option and indicate a reason for the cancellation.
5. Confirm the cancellation request and check your email inbox for an immediate cancellation receipt.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I cancel an order after it has shipped?
Once an order has shipped from our warehouse, it cannot be canceled directly. You can refuse delivery upon arrival or initiate a return within 30 days of delivery for a full refund.

### When will my refund be processed after canceling an order?
If your order is canceled before fulfillment, any payment hold or charge is refunded immediately. Funds typically appear on your original payment method within 3 to 5 business days.
