# How to View and Inspect Invoices

Access, review, and verify detailed invoice statements, sales tax breakdowns, and itemized receipts for all your Acme Store purchases.

## Steps

1. Log in to your Acme Store account at acme-store.example.com.
2. Open your account menu in the top-right corner and select 'Billing & Invoices'.
3. Filter records by date range or enter your invoice number (e.g., #INV-12345).
4. Click 'View Details' on the selected invoice to inspect item prices, discounts, and applied taxes.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long are past invoices accessible in my account?
All digital invoices and purchase records remain accessible in your Acme Store account history for up to 7 years.

### What should I do if an invoice contains incorrect billing information?
Contact our billing department at billing@acme-store.example.com with your invoice number (#INV-12345) and requested corrections for a revised statement.
