# How to Edit Your Account Information

Keep your personal profile, email address, phone number, and communication preferences current in your Acme Store account settings.

## Steps

1. Sign in to your Acme Store account at acme-store.example.com.
2. Click on your profile avatar in the upper-right corner and select 'Account Settings'.
3. Under the 'Personal Details' section, click 'Edit' next to your name, contact phone number, or email.
4. Update the relevant information in the form fields.
5. Click 'Save Changes' to apply your updates. If you changed your email address, confirm the verification link sent to your new address.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Will editing my account profile update existing pending orders?
No. Updating account details only affects future transactions. To update a shipping address on an open order, modify the order directly under 'Order History'.

### Why was I prompted to re-enter my password when saving changes?
To protect your security, changes to sensitive profile details like email address or password require re-entering your current password.
