# How to File a Customer Complaint

We take service quality and customer satisfaction seriously. Learn how to lodge a formal complaint and escalate issues to our dedicated dispute resolution team.

## Steps

1. Gather all pertinent information, including your order number (#ACME-12345), dates of interaction, photos of damaged goods, and a brief description of the issue.
2. Visit the complaint portal at acme-store.example.com/support/complaints or email complaints@acme-store.example.com.
3. Fill out the dispute intake form with your contact details and attach any supporting documentation.
4. Submit the complaint to receive a dedicated case number (#CASE-54321) within 2 hours.
5. A senior resolution specialist will investigate your case and provide an official response within 1 business day.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I escalate my complaint if I am unhappy with the initial response?
Yes. You can reply directly to your case manager requesting supervisor review, or call 1-800-555-ACME and ask for the Customer Advocacy Team.

### Is there an external arbitration option for unresolved disputes?
If a resolution cannot be reached through our claims team, you may request formal mediation in accordance with Acme Store terms of service.
