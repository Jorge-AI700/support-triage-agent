# Cancellation Fees and Policy

Acme Store maintains a customer-friendly cancellation policy. Understand when cancellations are free and the rare circumstances where restocking or processing fees apply.

## Steps

1. Visit acme-store.example.com and navigate to the 'Help Center' or 'Account Terms'.
2. Select 'Cancellation Policy' under the Order Management category.
3. Verify the classification of your items (standard consumer goods vs. custom-manufactured merchandise).
4. Check your order status under 'Order History' to confirm eligibility for no-fee cancellation.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does Acme Store charge fees for canceling standard orders?
No. There are never any fees or penalties for canceling standard orders prior to warehouse dispatch and shipment.

### Do customized or special-order products carry a cancellation fee?
Customized or engraved items canceled after production has begun may be subject to a 15% material restocking fee as disclosed at checkout.
