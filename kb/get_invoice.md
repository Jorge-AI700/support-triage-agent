# How to Download or Print an Invoice

Obtain official PDF copies of invoices, receipts, and order summaries for accounting, tax reporting, or expense reimbursement.

## Steps

1. Sign in to your Acme Store account at acme-store.example.com.
2. Navigate to 'Order History' or 'Billing & Invoices' from your account menu.
3. Find the order corresponding to the desired invoice using your order number (#ACME-12345).
4. Click the 'Download Invoice (PDF)' or 'Print Receipt' link next to the order summary.
5. Save the downloaded PDF to your local device or print a physical copy for your records.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I obtain an invoice with company VAT or corporate tax details?
Yes. Add your business name and Tax/VAT ID in 'Account Settings' under 'Company Billing' before generating the invoice download.

### Can customer service email an invoice directly to my accountant?
Yes. Send a request to billing@acme-store.example.com mentioning your order number (#ACME-12345) and the recipient email address.
