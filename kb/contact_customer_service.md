# How to Contact Acme Store Customer Support

Find all official contact channels, operating hours, phone numbers, and email addresses to reach Acme Store customer care.

## Steps

1. Review our support channels to choose the fastest option for your inquiry: Phone, Live Chat, or Email.
2. For Phone Support, call toll-free at 1-800-555-ACME (1-800-555-2263) during customer service hours: Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM – 8:00 PM EST.
3. For Live Chat, visit acme-store.example.com and click the chat icon in the bottom-right corner for instant messaging.
4. For Email Support, send your detailed query to support@acme-store.example.com for a guaranteed response within 24 business hours.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is phone customer service completely free?
Yes. 1-800-555-ACME is a toll-free number accessible throughout the United States and Canada.

### How can I get help outside of standard business hours?
Outside of operating hours (8:00 AM – 8:00 PM EST), you can submit an email ticket at support@acme-store.example.com or browse self-service articles at acme-store.example.com/help.
